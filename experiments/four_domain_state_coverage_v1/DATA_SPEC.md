# Executable data specification

Gold is an explicit world plus a current discrete state. Generators and operations are in semantics.py; the independent evaluator.py imports neither. Fixed audit.jsonl includes all states, signs and structures before neural inference. CPU checks include literal date/stance tables, cardinal rays, inverse/complete cycles and positive grammar roundtrips. Independent human review has not occurred and is not claimed.

Time: E fixed; C=E-r. plus updates C+1,r−1; minus C−1,r+1. Planned/completed/cancelled status is immutable, independent of dates. Templates0/1 express relative date and fixed objective color;2 holds out lexical paraphrases;3 adds immutable absoluteE;4 adds relation to a fixed launch;5 adds a dated historical direct quote with its own participants and unchanged words. Core current text supplies relative anchor information; absolute externalC is gold metadata and is not a hidden model input. No indirect/free-indirect quote targets.

Space: x east, y north; north/east/south/west headings0/1/2/3. Forward dot(dx,dy),(fx,fy), right dot(dx,dy),(fy,−fx). Objects lie on a nonzero cardinal ray. Main observer position, object world coordinate and secondary observer heading remain fixed. plus clockwise90°, minus anticlockwise90°. Core relations front/right/back/left; alternate viewpoint syntax, held-out ahead/behind syntax; absolute direction, secondary observer relation, object-to-marker fixed north relation are challenges. No observer translation. Marker is defined at the object's y−1 coordinate, so its relation is independent of main heading.

Emotion: levels0..4 correspond strongly negative/negative/neutral/positive/strongly positive. Task-defined ordinal operation only. Focus header names the evaluator and object; two evaluators' opposing stances are possible. plus+1/minus−1 only when legal; boundary saturation absent. Non-target evaluator, objective color, names/objects fixed. Held-out lexemes/syntax are template2; explicit bounded negation scope, another object, and quote of a third person's view are3/4/5. No invented causal facts. This task changes target meaning, not a globally meaning-preserving restatement.

Person: three named participants A,B,C; fixed transfer agentA, patientB, ownerC. Statej speaker=participants[j], listener=participants[(j+1)%3]. plus/minus cycles this context. Subject/object/possessive references resolve identities before selecting I/me/my, you/you/your, or unambiguous proper-name third-person forms. Subject–verb agreement is scored. Third-person names avoid irreversible gender ambiguity in core; explicit reflexives have a local subject binder. Quote speakerB/listenerC and historical quoted words remain fixed. Ambiguous third-person pronouns, plurality and sets are deferred and not represented as completed tests. Template1 changes clause structure;2 is held-out recipient construction;3 reflexive/owner binding;4 historical quote;5 fixed multi-participant relation.

No surface text from a world's trajectory crosses its split. Before split, world keys discard irrelevant dates/coordinates and reject overlapping template0 states, preventing easy core duplicate leakage. Shared IID templates are declared. Challenge templates never occur in atomic training, so failures are accompanied by their own natural atomic/reconstruction controls. Symbol grammar explicitly lists fields and has its own independently fitted editor. All hashes are in data/manifest.json.
