# Absolute-heading probe in the original spatial study

Recorded after all identity probes finished. Original probe fitting, labels and scores are retained, without rerunning or relabeling them.

The original study's `current_state` label is absolute heading0/1/2/3. Its natural core text supplies the object's observer-relative relation, observer identity and color/quantity. It supplies neither the fixed world-coordinate ray nor absolute observer heading. In a new content world, relative relation alone does not determine absolute heading: heading also depends on the world ray, which is gold metadata rather than allowed core model input. Content uniqueness does not make that missing spatial variable observable.

Consequently, a near-chance original-space absolute-heading probe is not evidence that the observable current front/right/back/left relation was erased. Its absolute-state train/test classification also contains an arbitrary new-world ray association. This is a limitation of the label and input specification, separate from representation-source transfer and historical-state correlation.

The independently rerun `space_relation_confirmation_v1` probe labels front/right/back/left, an observable variable of the controlled core text. Its results are the primary spatial current-semantic-state probe evidence. Across P/Q/U sources, the same labels and fixed P-only train fit are used. They remain descriptive, correlate with the single preferred incoming history, and do not establish editor use or causality. The original absolute-heading probe remains archived and explicitly named in the report.
