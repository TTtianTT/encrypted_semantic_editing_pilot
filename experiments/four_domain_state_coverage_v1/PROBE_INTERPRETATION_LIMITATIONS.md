# Probe interpretation limits

Recorded on 2026-10-03 before the scheduled named-identity probe array starts. This is an interpretation audit, not a change to the frozen fitting protocol, labels, caches or hyperparameters in MECHANISM_EXTENSION_PLAN.md.

Labels come from the structured intended current state. Overall accuracy includes source representations whose current decoding fails the controlled qualification; it must not be described as accuracy on the model's actual decoded state. The current-correct subset and the current-correct/next-failed subset are reported separately. Probe accuracy alone does not establish that a variable is used by the editor.

Each current state uses one preferred incoming direction, with a reverse incoming edge where required at a boundary. Fit and cross-source test caches have depth one and share this history convention. A state label is therefore correlated with the precursor state and incoming operation. A successful linear readout may exploit a precursor/history cue; testing on another editor source does not remove this correlation. These probes establish prediction of labels in these frozen trajectories, not a distinct representation of current state independent of history. Cross-depth and counterbalanced-history probe tests were not executed.

Named subject/object information may remain in unchanged input spans. Readability does not establish correct contextual binding, anchor selection, or preservation of every non-target fact. Space target-subject and anchor-owner labels coincide; emotion target-subject and scope-owner labels coincide. Their two reported columns are not independent discoveries. Time's core external anchor-owner label is constant; no classifier is fitted for it, and no quote-versus-external-anchor ownership probe is available.

The identity failure subsets join fixed-source continuation matrices to the exact frozen P/Q/U source caches. They do not probe every receiver's self-generated multi-step trajectory. Matching a world/state/text label does not establish that a reviewed trajectory uses that same hidden vector: a reverse or later-depth history can differ from the preferred incoming edge in the probe cache.

All participant/object outputs retain the full fixed class inventory. Train/test class frequencies, train-majority and train-frequency-random baselines, and absent train classes are reported. Failure-subset tables retain operation-row counts and deduplicate identical source/world/current-state representations for a second readout comparison. Neither repeated operations nor repeated labels are independent samples. A weak linear probe cannot establish that information is absent, and a strong probe cannot identify the cause of an editing failure. No subspace intervention or causal mechanism test is performed.
