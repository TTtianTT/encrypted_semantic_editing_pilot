This is a protocol correction after the initial study began, not an independently motivated replication.

Current state is relative bearing: 0=front,1=right,2=back,3=left. Coordinates have x east and y north, facing north/east/south/west indexed0/1/2/3. For the object's cardinal world ray q and state r, observer facing is (q-r) mod4. plus rotates facing clockwise90 degrees and therefore maps r to r-1 mod4; minus maps r to r+1. Four equal-signed operations restore facing and relative semantics. Both observer positions and all object coordinates stay fixed. The other observer's facing stays fixed.
