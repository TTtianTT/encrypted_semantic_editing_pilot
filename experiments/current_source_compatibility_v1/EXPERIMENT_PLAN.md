## Decisions and statistics

Three seeds primary; mean and min/max next. Retention requires natural macro drop≤2pp separately per seed and still lists old successes lost. Direct repair primary metric full2, length transfer full3–5. Stable R advantage requires strictly positive R−F across all three seeds in the specified source/template/length; ties do not establish advantage. Report world-cluster paired bootstrap2000 draws for R−F and F−N using shared worlds, all their sequences together. Intervals describe fixed-model world sampling, exclude full training randomness. Correct-prefix continuation and incorrect-prefix recovery are separate; recovery never proves correct-current repair. Improvement at3 but near-zero5 is local length transfer, not resolution of long chains.
