\section{Deviations}\label{app:deviations}
% per round, from DEVIATIONS.md, AMENDMENT_*.md and ERRATUM_*.md of each experiment directory
Each experiment directory holds a \texttt{DEVIATIONS.md} with every difference from its plan (dated, with reason and impact, and marked
as before or after the lock), and, where needed, an \texttt{AMENDMENT} (a change of what is run, decided by the project lead) or an
\texttt{ERRATUM} (a correction of a report statement). None changes a locked verdict. We summarise those that matter for interpretation.

\paragraph{v1.}
AMENDMENT~01 changed the opening geometry gate after its results were seen (data-driven): the time-domain geometry was confounded by the
token length of the state phrases, so the geometry test moved to the single-token weekday and month domains, and one condition whose
direction contradicted the others was dropped. All confirmatory predictions of v1 were still locked before any editing result. Other
deviations are numerical (eigenvalues on CPU) or bookkeeping.

\paragraph{v2.}
After the lock, the binding template was found not to be reconstructible by BART (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}); AMENDMENT~01 declared the
two affected predictions invalid, kept the others, and moved the retest and two optional units to v3. The v3 audit later found that
part of v2's confirmation worlds rendered to texts already present in v1's train or dev split, because v2 deduplicated only within its
own confirmation set (footnote in Section~\ref{sec:setup}).

\paragraph{v3.}
Before the lock: the confirmation generator was changed to deduplicate against all historical renders, and a dev-only smoke test was
added. After the report, ERRATUM~01 corrected five statements: a long-chain effect on the scrambled T5Gemma mapping was a rank-threshold
effect; the fundamental's vote share depends on the displacement (small at one step, dominant at large ones); only ``$s_1$ is smallest''
holds in every cell, not an ordering of all $s_k$; the trained-editor failure was under-training rather than an unreachable solution; and
a premise of N1 was wrong.

\paragraph{v4.}
Operational definitions were written into the protocol before the lock without changing thresholds; the re-analysis of v1/v2 removed the
duplicated worlds found in v3. After the lock: figure formats, a JSONL reader fix (two failed jobs rerun unchanged) and descriptive
confidence intervals added to the report.

\paragraph{v5.}
Before the lock: the confirmation worlds of the multi-template domains were deduplicated at the text level; v4 gates and fits were
reused where the plan allowed; the multi-template base-operator gate removed the whole unit (Q18 not evaluable). The post-hoc
reproduction of the earlier analysis fixed the aggregation level of each reported number.

\paragraph{v6.}
Thirteen entries, all before the lock. Text-level deduplication against all earlier renders exhausted the held-out templates with few
fields, so the multi-template conclusions rest on the templates with more fields (Appendix~\ref{app:data}). The synthetic self-test of
the binding editors was rebuilt (the first construction was unsolvable), its one-layer criterion was set at the provable one-layer floor,
and it needed a larger learning rate and batch than the plan to learn the two-hop lookup; the real binding editors kept the planned
settings. The context calibration fell on the boundary (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}); a first lock was discarded before commit because
a floating-point tie had moved the weight off zero. Editors trained with the same seed and batch sequence as an earlier round turned out
bit-identical to it, so some cells of the context unit are duplicates (listed in the analysis). After the lock, AMENDMENT~01 only added
descriptive Wilson intervals for the report; no locked verdict changed.

\paragraph{v7.}
Before the lock, smoke and dev runs found and fixed code errors (an import of the previous round's library, a device mismatch, a vote model
loaded for binding domains, a mismatch between dev cells and dev shards); the template-0 text space was exhausted, so the sixth round's
confirmation set was reused for template-0 domains (text reused, recorded); the duplicate-cell detection of the post-hoc analysis was
replaced by the sixth round's list. No change after the lock affected a verdict.

\paragraph{v8.}
Before the lock: two exploratory units were added by the project lead (the operator-gain analysis of Section~\ref{sec:scope} and the
binding write-rank test); the template-0 sets of the sixth round were reused for the new models (first evaluation for these models);
missing chat templates were added at the pinned model revisions; one attention kernel was switched off for the two smallest T5Gemma sizes
because it had no execution plan on our GPUs; the decoder-only layer rule was changed by the lead from the shallowest to the median of the
layers that tie on the single-step check (layer 2 sits just above the token embedding). After the lock: AMENDMENT~01 (exploratory parts
are run in full; no part was in fact skipped), AMENDMENT~02 (the binding residue was re-measured on random-direction chains, because the
first probe could not separate the previous from the current name), AMENDMENT~03 (the post-lock sanity gate G8-0, not run as a separate
step, was computed from the same chains; it passes).

\paragraph{v9.}
Before the lock: template-0 texts reused (first evaluation for every new editor); the discriminability pre-check was operationalised by the
lead as a latent simulation on development worlds (a condition the simulation puts on the wrong side is dropped; the harmonic rotation,
whose readout is exact by construction, is descriptive); unrolled editors trained with the decoder objective alone that never reached their
own horizon are excluded from the horizon test, which is decided on admitted editors only; T5Gemma weekday confirmation encodings were made
in this round; one attention kernel was switched off for the decoder-only candidates. The second decoder-only family was replaced in turn
(Qwen2.5 paraphrases when repeating; OLMo-2 and Phi-{3.5} split the state words; SmolLM2 failed the full reconstruction gate), so the
decoder-only unit was evaluated on Gemma 2 2B weekday only. After the lock: AMENDMENT~01 (the lead approved the compute beyond the cap;
unused weights deleted) and AMENDMENT~02 (the analysis skips a model without an evaluable combination).
