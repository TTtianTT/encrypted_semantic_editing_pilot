\section{Implementation details}\label{app:implementation}
% definitions for CL1-CL14; constants are design choices fixed in the plans, not results
\paragraph{Backbones.}
BART-base and T5Gemma 2B--2B (UL2, instruction-tuned), both loaded from pinned revisions whose file hashes are checked at the start of
every job; T5Gemma runs in bf16, editors in float32. Encoder and decoder are frozen; decoding is greedy.

\paragraph{Token-wise editors.}
A token-wise editor adds a low-rank residual at every non-pad position $i$: $h_i' = h_i + b + UVh_i$ with $V \in \mathbb{R}^{r\times d}$
initialised with small Gaussian entries and $U$ initialised at zero, so training starts at the identity. C1 and C3 use rank $16$, one
editor per operation (plus, minus), AdamW with learning rate $10^{-3}$, no weight decay, gradient clipping at $1$, and $600$ updates;
seeds $42$, $43$, $44$. C1 minimises the decoder cross-entropy of the target sentence after one step. C3 adds the canonical $L_2$ term
$\lVert T(E(x)) - E(x\pm1)\rVert^2$ averaged over non-pad positions and divided by the mean ideal-write energy
$\lVert E(x\pm1) - E(x)\rVert^2$ of the training worlds.

\paragraph{Unrolled training.}
In unrolled variants (U4) each update draws a path type (plus$^S$, minus$^S$, alternating) and a length $S$ uniformly from
$\{1,\dots,4\}$, applies the editors $S$ times from cached encodings and averages the per-step loss (cross-entropy, plus the $L_2$ term
for C3-U4); gradients flow through the whole chain and both editors are updated together. With $S=1$ the loss equals the single-step loss
(checked to $10^{-6}$ in the self-test).

\paragraph{Closed-form operators.}
RRR-$r$ fits an ordinary least-squares map $\hat B$ from source to target encodings over all tokens of the training pairs, then projects
it on the top $r$ right singular vectors of the fitted update. FF-$r$ (fundamental-first) differs in one respect: its output subspace is
forced to contain the slot plane of the fundamental, $Q = \mathrm{orth}([Q_1, V_\perp])$, where $V_\perp$ are the top $r-2$ singular
vectors of the fitted update with plane~1 removed. The slot-centred variants of v6 (S-RRR-$r$, $E_{\mathrm{all}}$) apply the same fits to
the slot position only.

\paragraph{Harmonic planes, vote model and frozen-plane surgery.}
The canonical slot encodings are averaged over training worlds after removing each world's mean; their discrete Fourier components give
the planes $B_k$. The vote model reads a state from a slot vector $h_s$ as
$P(y\mid h) \propto \exp(-\sum_k \lambda_k \lVert B_k^\top(h_s - c_w(y))\rVert^2)$ with $\lambda_k \ge 0$ fitted by maximum likelihood on
decodable dev probes; the equal-weight and fundamental-only models are its controls. Frozen-plane surgery $F_S$ applies the base
operator and then restores the planes in $S$ to their values at the start of the chain.

\paragraph{Latent prediction.}
Given an editor and the encoded confirmation worlds, the latent predictor applies the editor along each locked path without decoding and
reads the slot with the locked vote model at every step; a world is predicted composable if every step's readout equals the target.
The naive control predicts composability from decoded success at step $1$; the per-cell majority class is a reference.

\paragraph{Binding editors.}
Tok-U is token-wise with rank $32$. Att1-U adds one attention head ($d_a = 64$, $W_o$ zero-initialised, so training starts at Tok-U);
Att2-U uses two heads. The two-layer editor Att2L of v6 stacks two such layers, each with zero-initialised output, on top of a rank-$32$
token-wise term. The oracle-routing editor OR (diagnostic only) writes the value of the next listed participant into the slot.
In the sixth round all binding editors are trained with the unrolled $L_2$ objective (AdamW, learning rate $10^{-3}$, batch $16$):
OR-U4 and Att2L-U4 on unrolls of up to four steps for $4000$ updates, Att2L-U8 and Att1-U8 on unrolls of up to eight steps for
$8000$ updates; admission required dev single-step success of at least $95\%$ on name3 and bind3fix.


\paragraph{Eighth round.}
T5Gemma 9B-9B, L-L and B-B (UL2, instruction-tuned) use the same wrapper as T5Gemma 2B-2B and are pinned to fixed revisions. Rank grids
are $r \in \{N-4,\dots,N, 16\}$ plus the predicted threshold and two below it, with FF-$r$ for the rank-deficient $r$. New editors use
seeds 50--52 (C1: 600 updates; C3-U4: 1200 updates, $S \le 4$); all were admitted at their first length. For the decoder-only model
(Gemma 2 2B, instruction-tuned) the chat prompt asks the model to repeat the sentence; the latent is the residual stream after layer
$\ell$ at the month token of the prompt; an edit replaces that vector during the prefill and every later position is recomputed from layer
$\ell+1$; generation is greedy and success is an exact repeat of the target sentence. Layer $\ell$ is the median of the layers whose
single-step success with S-RRR-16 is maximal on dev (layers 2--18 tie; later layers degrade and the last two fail). The decoder-trained
slot editor is trained through the upper layers with teacher-forced generation (600 updates, seeds 50--52).
\paragraph{Compute.}
All jobs ran on single NVIDIA B300 GPUs through Slurm, at most two at a time. GPU hours per round (run ledgers): \CLmetaGpuVone{},
\CLmetaGpuVtwo{}, \CLmetaGpuVthree{}, \CLmetaGpuVfour{}, \CLmetaGpuVfive{}, \CLmetaGpuVsix{}, \CLmetaGpuVseven{}, \CLmetaGpuVeight{} and \CLmetaGpuVnine{} for the first to the ninth round, \CLmetaGpuTotal{} in
total.
