# Third-party data and materials

The StylePTB data included in `data/` and quoted in experiment outputs originates from **Yiwei Lyu, Paul Pu Liang, Hai Pham, Eduard Hovy, Barnabas Poczos, Ruslan Salakhutdinov, and Louis-Philippe Morency**, *StylePTB: A Compositional Benchmark for Fine-grained Controllable Text Style Transfer*, NAACL 2021.

- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.171/
- Repository: https://github.com/lvyiwei1/StylePTB
- Revision: `0c24befeb80098307d7bdff6c5adaf04529fa36f`
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original license statement: `data/StylePTB_LICENSE`.

The experiment filters, groups, deduplicates, and samples these data. Manifests and provenance audits record these modifications. Model-generated outputs and automatic evaluations are experimental artifacts, not author-endorsed gold annotations.

Downloaded papers and third-party implementation excerpts used during the literature review are excluded from Git. Their sources are linked in `related_work.md`. Pretrained BART/Qwen weights and the spaCy model wheel are also excluded; their respective licenses continue to apply when obtained from their original sources. Dependency licenses are not replaced by this notice. No new repository-wide license is assigned by this upload.
